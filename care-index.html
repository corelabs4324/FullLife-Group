Ways We Can Help

Start with what is happening now.

You do not need to know the service label before you call. Tell us what the person needs, what has changed and the timeframe. We will help identify the most suitable next step.

01

Planned respite

A supported short stay that gives participants a change of environment, builds confidence and gives families and carers time to recharge.

Typical stay: 2–14 nights
02

Short-term accommodation

Accessible accommodation with tailored daily support for planned stays, changing circumstances or a temporary need for additional assistance.

Flexible, needs-led stays
03

Hospital transition

A calm step between hospital and home when someone is medically ready to leave but needs suitable accommodation and coordinated support.

Referral-led pathway
04

Medium-term accommodation

A stable interim setting while a participant waits for a confirmed long-term home, home modifications or another permanent arrangement.

Interim accommodation
05

Community & daily living

Practical, person-led support for routines, appointments, activities, relationships and participation beyond the accommodation setting.

Individual support plans

How It Works

A careful pathway, without unnecessary hurdles.

Our online enquiry collects only basic contact information. Personal, health and support details can be discussed privately with the Care team.

01

Connect

Tell us who is enquiring, the broad need and preferred contact method.

02

Understand

We discuss goals, support needs, timing, funding and any immediate considerations.

03

Explore

Where appropriate, we arrange a visit and confirm whether the setting and support are suitable.

04

Plan

We agree the stay, support approach, responsibilities and next steps before arrival.

Aurora — Our Purpose-Built Care Setting

Aurora Sunshine Coast

A welcoming accommodation and wellness environment in Kuluin, designed around accessibility, dignity and everyday life.

Aurora brings private accessible suites together with shared living, activity and wellness spaces. The setting supports planned respite, short and medium-term accommodation and suitable transition pathways as services are commissioned.

Finding The Right Fit

Suitability comes before availability.

Every person's circumstances are different. A stay is subject to an individual discussion, assessment of support needs, funding arrangements, service availability and safe service delivery.

Full Life Care is not an emergency service, hospital or clinical facility. If someone is in immediate danger or needs urgent medical care, call 000.
